About this preview

Prayer times use standard astronomical formulas, adjust automatically for the high-latitude case (long summer nights), and can be fine-tuned by up to 3 hours per prayer above. Qur'an text, translations, and the reciter list are always pulled live from an open Qur'an API, so they stay accurate and keep growing. The adhan plays automatically at each prayer while this app is open, using the muezzin you pick above. "Play full surah" automatically chains individual ayahs together for reciters that don't have a single combined recording.

Two honest limits: a browser tab can't reliably play full-length audio or fire notifications once it's fully closed — the native app build handles real background prayer alerts. And a true lock-screen/home-screen widget isn't possible for a web app at all; that would need the native app compiled with a platform-specific widget extension (see its README).
