Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026
Mawaqit doesn't have user accounts and doesn't sell your data. Your location is used only to calculate prayer times and qibla direction, on your own device — it isn't sent to us or stored on any server we control. The app shows banner ads (unless you've purchased "Remove Ads" in the native app) and offers optional push notifications, both handled by the third-party providers named below — everything else stays entirely on your device.

Information we collect

Location. With your permission, the app reads your device's location to calculate accurate prayer times and the qibla direction for where you are. This calculation happens entirely on your device. We do not receive, store, or have access to your location — there is no server of ours in this data path at all.

Push notification identifier. If you opt in to "Announcements," our notification provider, OneSignal, assigns your device an identifier so we can send occasional messages (e.g. a du'a) to everyone who's opted in. See OneSignal's privacy policy. You can opt out at any time from the in-app toggle or your device/browser notification settings.

Advertising identifier (native app only). The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob on two screens, unless you've purchased "Remove Ads." AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads. See Google's ad policy. You can reset or limit this at the OS level, and iOS separately asks your permission before this identifier can be used.

Purchase information (native app only). If you buy "Remove Ads," the purchase is processed by Apple/Google and recorded by our billing provider, RevenueCat, so your ad-free status can be restored on other devices. We don't receive your payment details — only that a purchase was made. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Nothing else. The app has no account creation, no login, and doesn't collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or any other personal information beyond what's described above. Your prayer-tracking history, bookmarks, and preferences are stored only in local storage on your own device and are never transmitted to us.

Third-party content services

To show Qur'an text/audio, hadith text, and place names, the app fetches data directly from these public services when you use those features. Requests go straight from your device to them — we don't proxy or log these requests ourselves:

  • Al Quran Cloud API (alquran.cloud) — Qur'an text, translations
  • Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network) — Qur'an recitation audio
  • fawazahmed0/hadith-api (via jsdelivr CDN) — hadith text
  • praytimes.org — adhan audio recordings
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — converts coordinates to a place name; only used if you search a city or use device location

Each operates under its own terms; none of them receive any information from Mawaqit that identifies you personally beyond the specific request being made (e.g. "fetch Surah 2").

Notifications

Two separate kinds: personal reminders (prayer alerts, du'a reminders, adhan) are scheduled by you, for you, and never leave your device. Announcements are the only kind that come from us, via OneSignal, and only if you've opted in.

Children's privacy

Mawaqit doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and has no age-gated features since there's no account system to begin with.

Data retention & deletion

Since we don't hold your personal data on any server, there's nothing for us to delete on request. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app (or clear this site's browsing data) — this clears all local preferences, bookmarks, and prayer history. To stop announcements, opt out in Settings or your notification settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
